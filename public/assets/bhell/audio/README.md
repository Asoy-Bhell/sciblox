# Offline Tagalog narration

These speech clips are generated locally from authored SciBlox Tagalog captions using [Meta MMS TTS Tagalog](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-tgl), by Meta AI. Model and derived voice assets are provided under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Their license applies separately from the application code: these included voice assets are for noncommercial use and must retain attribution. No Siri or Dola recordings or cloned voices are used.

The model revision, caption-to-file mapping and sample rate are recorded in `src/guide/tagalog-audio.json`. Generated speech is compressed to mono MP3 and played from the installed app. No model, download, account, or internet connection is needed during playback. English narration and explicitly selected device voices still use the phone's installed offline speech engine.

Generation uses a fixed random seed (23); captions retain genetics notation while the spoken text expands DNA and XP. Playback is slightly faster (1.05×) without changing pitch. Listen to the voice preview on target devices before classroom use; synthetic pronunciation of scientific notation can vary.
